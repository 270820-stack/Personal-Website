About me

Art, history,
and culture.

I’m a student at Hong Kong International School with more than a decade of visual-arts practice in painting, drawing, and woodblock printing. My work brings together cultural research, graphic design, video editing, and skills in Python, C++, and UI/UX. I’m interested in how aesthetics and technology can help us share and preserve culture.

Fluent in English, Mandarin, and Cantonese, I value clear organization and collaboration. I’ve led art-education projects, coordinated student portrait-making teams, and created visual content for Goldstone Museum. I’ve also interviewed professors and produced short videos about their careers for WeChat, Rednote, and Bilibili.

Based in Hong Kong, I explore art and history through research, exhibitions, and digital media. From Weave-a-World’s cultural dyes and textiles to a photo-editing app prototype developed with a team at Adobe, I aim to combine visual craft and technical skills to help more people experience and appreciate culture.

My art journey

Journey

Eleven years of
looking and making.

I’ve been an artist for 11 years. From painting outdoors with my brother to looking closely at works in museums, art has always been part of how I explore the world.

My brother and I sold 90 of our paintings and donated the proceeds to the Adream Foundation. Making art became a way to give back, as well as a way to express ourselves.

Along the way, I developed an art portfolio, managed an Instagram account with 90 followers, and created an online art exhibit for 100 people. I also completed five commissions totaling $30,000.
